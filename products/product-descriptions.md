## GPSR Product Page Requirements

Under GPSR (General Product Safety Regulation), every product listing must include:

1. **Manufacturer name and contact**: [Supplier name], [City], China
2. **EU Responsible Person**: [YOUR COMPANY LEGAL NAME], [Address], Ireland — hello@[brandname].com
3. **Product identifier**: Use the SKU codes from the CSV (e.g., MJ-ALP-BLK-M)
4. **Material composition**: Already included in descriptions above
5. **Country of origin**: Made in China (this is legally required — don't hide it)
6. **Safety warnings**: "Not suitable as personal protective equipment (PPE). Designed for recreational skiing in normal conditions."

### Where to place GPSR info on Shopify:
- Add a collapsible section titled "Product Information & Compliance" at the bottom of each product page
- Include manufacturer info, EU responsible person, material composition, and care instructions
