# [BRANDNAME] Brand Guidelines

## Brand Positioning

**One-liner**: Affordable premium ski wear for young Europeans who want to look great on the slopes without overpaying.

**Brand Promise**: Performance ski wear at honest prices. No middlemen, no markups — just quality gear designed for real skiers.

## Tone of Voice

### We are:
- **Confident** — We know our product is good. We don't apologize for being affordable.
- **Adventurous** — We celebrate the thrill of skiing, not the luxury of it.
- **Honest** — No "revolutionary fabric technology" BS. We say what it is.
- **Inclusive** — Skiing is for everyone, not just the wealthy.

### We are NOT:
- Cheap or discount-focused — We never say "cheap ski wear". We say "honest prices".
- Luxury or exclusive — We don't gatekeep. Everyone deserves good gear.
- Overly technical — We explain specs simply. "Keeps you dry in heavy snow" > "10,000mm hydrostatic head rating".
- Corporate — We sound like a friend who knows about skiing, not a brand memo.

### Example Copy Comparisons

| Instead of... | We say... |
|--------------|-----------|
| "Premium quality at an affordable price" | "Built for the slopes, not the price tag" |
| "Our revolutionary waterproof technology" | "Stays dry in heavy snow. Tested. Proven." |
| "Shop our collection" | "Gear up for your next trip" |
| "Limited time offer" | "Early bird pricing — ends Friday" |
| "Best ski jacket 2026" | "The jacket your wallet and the mountain both approve of" |

## Logo Guidelines

### Requirements
- Must work on white AND dark backgrounds
- Must be legible at small sizes (mobile, favicon)
- Should include a wordmark (text-based logo)
- Optional: simple icon/symbol that works standalone

### Recommended Style
- Clean, sans-serif wordmark
- Lowercase or mixed case (avoid ALL CAPS — feels aggressive)
- Consider a subtle mountain/snow element integrated into a letter

### Where to Create
- **DIY**: Canva Pro (free trial) — use their logo maker
- **Budget**: Fiverr — €30-80 for a logo package
- **Better**: 99designs — €250+ for a design contest

---

## Photography Style

### Product Photos (for website)
- Clean white or light grey background
- Show the full garment, front and back
- At least one detail shot (zipper, pocket, hood)
- Lifestyle shots: person wearing the jacket outdoors (even without snow)

### Social Media Content
- Natural, unpolished feel — TikTok audience distrusts overly produced content
- Good lighting but not studio-perfect
- Show real people, real settings
- Text overlays are OK and expected on TikTok
