## How It Works

1. **Order placed** — You'll receive an order confirmation email immediately.
2. **Processing** — Your order is prepared and quality-checked within 1-3 business days.
3. **Shipped** — You'll receive a tracking number via email once your order is dispatched.
4. **In transit** — Track your package in real-time using the link in your email.
5. **Delivered** — Your gear arrives at your door, ready for the slopes.

## Tracking Your Order

Every order comes with a tracking number. You'll receive it by email within 3 business days of placing your order. You can also check your order status anytime by logging into your account on our website.
