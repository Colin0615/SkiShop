## Our Return Policy

We want you to love your gear. If you're not completely satisfied, you can return any item within **14 days** of delivery for a full refund — no questions asked.

This is your right under EU consumer protection law, and we fully honour it.

## How to Return

1. **Contact us** at hello@[brandname].com with your order number and reason for return.
2. **We'll send you** a return shipping label and instructions within 24 hours.
3. **Pack the item** in its original packaging (or similar protective packaging).
4. **Ship it back** using the provided label.
5. **Refund processed** within 5-7 business days of receiving your return.

## Return Conditions

- Items must be returned within **14 days** of delivery.
- Items must be **unworn and in original condition** — tags attached, no stains, no damage.
- Tried it on at home? That's fine. Took it skiing? That's not returnable (unless defective).
- Items must be returned in their original packaging or equivalent protective packaging.

## Refund Details

- Refunds are issued to your original payment method.
- Original shipping costs are refunded if you're returning the entire order.
- If you're returning part of an order, original shipping costs are not refunded.
- Refunds typically appear in your account within 5-7 business days, depending on your bank.

## Defective or Wrong Items

Received a defective item or the wrong product? We're sorry — contact us immediately and we'll:

- Send you a **free replacement**, or
- Issue a **full refund** including return shipping costs

You don't need to wait — contact us and we'll resolve it within 48 hours.

## EU Warranty

Under EU law, all products come with a **2-year legal guarantee**. If a product develops a defect within 2 years of delivery that was not caused by wear and tear or misuse, you are entitled to a free repair, replacement, or refund.

## Exchanges

We don't currently offer direct exchanges. To get a different size or colour:

1. Return the original item (free return).
2. Place a new order for the item you want.

This ensures the fastest processing time.
