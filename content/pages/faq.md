## Product & Quality

### Are your jackets really waterproof?
Yes. Our jackets are rated at 10,000mm+ waterproof and 5,000+ g/m² breathability. This is comparable to jackets from brands charging €300-500. All seams are fully sealed to prevent water penetration.

### What materials do you use?
Our products are primarily made from high-quality polyester with DWR (Durable Water Repellent) coating. Each product page lists the exact fabric composition. We use YKK zippers on all our garments.

### How warm are your jackets?
Our jackets are designed for active skiing in typical European resort conditions (-5 to -15 degrees Celsius). They work best as part of a layering system: base layer + mid layer + our jacket. For extreme cold, add a thicker mid layer.

### How do I care for my ski wear?
- Machine wash cold (30 degrees), gentle cycle
- Use a technical wash (like Nikwax Tech Wash), not regular detergent
- Do not use fabric softener — it damages waterproof coatings
- Tumble dry low to reactivate the DWR coating
- Do not iron or dry clean

Detailed care instructions are included on the garment label and each product page.
